## Teaching arc

### 001-005: Read a shadow
- 001: one DOT, one direction.
- 002: direction selection.
- 003: persistent accumulation.
- 004: multiple SOCKETS.
- 005: crossing orthogonal shadows.

### 006-010: Grouping is an action
- 006: two separated DOTs can be compressed into one SHOT.
- 007: aligned DOTs deliberately create a zero hole and a double shadow beyond.
- 008: same SOCKET geometry as 007, but grouping is wrong; the DOTs must be separated across SHOTs.
- 009: mix a SOLO SHOT and GROUP SHOT.
- 010: first explicit "clear vs PAR" compression lesson.

Stage 007 -> 008 is the first signature AFTERLUX AHA.

### 011-015: Reverse decomposition
The player starts reading TARGET as a sum of SHOT masks rather than simply drawing shadows.

### 016-019: Four-direction logic
Unlock N/S/W/E and increase overlap ambiguity without adding a new physical gimmick.

### 020: First 5x5 exam
A compact four-direction PAR 4 puzzle that combines grouping, overlap, and zero-hole reasoning.
